NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 • Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map

Q1. Target Martian Region & Scientific Objective Lower northwestern flank of Mount Sharp (Aeolis Mons, Gale Crater). The pressurized EVA Rover stages at WP-01 (Lower Murray Plain, -2440 m, 3.1° slope, 325 tiu) because unconsolidated sand (135 tiu) and a steep 16.8° scarp ahead block wheeled vehicles. Astronauts execute a 940 m foot traverse across the ancient lakebed-to-sulfate transition to sample the Boxwork Ridges (WP-04, -2385 m)—polygonal cemented fractures formed by late-stage subsurface groundwater.
Q2. Strict Integration of the 5 Mandatory NASA Data Sources (Confidence ≥ 95%)
  • 1. NASA Mars Trek (trek.nasa.gov/mars): Topographic contours (-2450 m to -2375 m), critical 16.8° scarp hazard, 8.5° northern shelter ridge, thermal inertia trafficability (325 tiu bedrock vs. 135 tiu sand), and spectral mineralogy layers.
  • 2. USGS Astrogeology Interactive Maps: Stratigraphic contacts between the Murray Formation (Mudstone), Stimson Sandstone Unconformity, and Sulfate-Bearing Unit.
  • 3. Curiosity's Proposed Path Up Mount Sharp: Reference rover ascent trajectory from clay/hematite strata to the Boxwork sulfate ridges and surface radiation/environmental baseline.
  • 4. NASA Worldview (worldview.earthdata.nasa.gov): Multi-layer atmospheric optical depth (Dust Tau τ) and visibility constraint architecture.
  • 5. Mars 2020 Multimedia Raw Images: Integrated as an Analog Surface Texture Library correlated with Gale Crater lithologies to train and prepare astronauts for boot traction and outcrop sampling at each station.
Q3. Astronaut Mobility, Safety & Dual Point-of-No-Return Architecture
  • Locked Departure PNR (1010 m) + Live Contingency Envelope: The nominal Point of No Return (1010 m) encompasses the primary WP-04 target (940 m) at departure and remains locked once the traverse starts; if a mid-traverse hazard (SEP storm, dust haze/storm, or overweight load) degrades O₂ safety margins below 940 m, an inner Contingency Walkback Limit appears and the autonomous pathplanner cuts off unreachable stations and reroutes the astronaut.
  • Dual EVA Routes: Automatic rerouting from the Primary EVA Route to the Northern Scarp Shelter Alternate Route (8.5° max grade) during Solar Energetic Particle (SEP) events, dust storms, or suit overweight states.
  • Gloved & Hands-Free Operation: Minimalist symbolic Helmet Visor HUD with dynamic terrain/dust rendering and audio chimes, a decluttered 16:9 Forearm Map Display, bilingual Voice Commands, and a single physical analog bezel button.
